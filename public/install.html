Bring a little support to your browser.

This hackathon prototype installs locally in Chrome or Edge. It is not yet published in a browser store.

Download the extension ZIP
  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you’ll keep.
  2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  3. Enable Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  4. Reload any existing web tabs. Open a form, focus a field, and press Alt+H. You can also open the extension’s toolbar popup and choose Hold this place.
  5. Switch tabs, then come back. Choose Continue to find your next field. Open the popup to inspect, edit or delete your place.

Optional AI

In extension Settings, enable Cloudflare AI and enter this website’s HTTPS origin (the address before any slash). Grant network access. For each saved place, inspect the exact AI payload, then choose Generate AI cue. Local-only is the default.

Good to know

Browser settings pages, extension stores, PDFs, cross-origin frames and some custom web components cannot be captured. The extension doesn’t restore typed answers or submit forms. Return cues depend on the page still having a recognizable structure. If a site is unusual, leave yourself a note.

Alt+H can conflict with browser or OS shortcuts. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts. All popup and banner controls work with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter and Space.
