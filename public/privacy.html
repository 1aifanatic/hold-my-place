Your place stays yours.

Local by default

The extension saves page URL, title, visible headings, field labels and filled/empty flags, focused label, scroll position, and your optional note in chrome.storage.local. It never reads field values into its stored capture, records keystrokes, takes screenshots, or sends analytics. The demo uses localStorage on this site. Browsing the hosted demo necessarily requests its files from Cloudflare.

Cloud AI is your choice

Only after you explicitly request an AI cue, titles, headings, labels, filled/empty flags, focused label, step and your note go to Cloudflare Workers AI. The preview shows the exact JSON. URLs, field values, screenshots, cookies and scroll coordinates are excluded. The Worker has no database and request-body logging is disabled. Cloudflare still processes network requests under its own policies; we do not promise zero provider retention.

Control and limitations

Places expire after 24 hours by default (configurable in the extension). Expired records are cleaned on access and by a minute timer while the browser runs. Edit notes, delete one place, or use Forget everything to wipe stored places and settings. The demo and extension have separate stores. Browser speech may use an operating-system or network speech service depending on your voice settings.

Titles, URLs, labels and notes may themselves contain sensitive information. A keyword guard blocks cloud AI on likely banking, health, payment and password-manager pages, but cannot recognize every sensitive site. Inspect the preview before sending. AI cues can be wrong; use This isn’t right to correct them. The tool is an accessibility prototype, not medical advice or a guarantee against duplicate submissions.

Optional continuous voice listening and automatic interruption suggestions are not enabled. This prototype has not yet been validated in a participant study or a full screen-reader audit.
