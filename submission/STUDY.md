# Small participant study — ready to run, not yet run

Do not fill results with targets or synthetic data. The PRD's +25 percentage-point completion and −50% time targets are hypotheses.

Recruit 5–8 adult volunteers, ideally including people who self-identify with attention or memory challenges. No diagnosis documentation is needed. Explain that this is a prototype, not treatment. Participation is optional, can stop at any time, and uses fictional data only. Ask permission before recording; default to anonymous observation without recording. Keep consent/contact data separate from results. Ask participants what accommodations or breaks they want.

Five interview questions:

1. How often do you lose your place during an online task?
2. What do you do when you return after an interruption?
3. What information would help you resume first?
4. Would a visible reminder, spoken reminder, or your own note help most?
5. What should be saved, and when should it be forgotten?

Protocol: provide two equally sized five-step practice tasks (licence renewal and library registration), using fictional details. Randomly assign half the participants to extension-first and half to control-first; reverse task/condition pairings across participants. Use a five-minute neutral interruption at the same task stage. Avoid practising the exact same form twice. Start the resumption timer when the task becomes visible, stop on the first correct action. Record failure to resume separately rather than dropping it. Offer rest between tasks. Do not withhold essential accessibility tools in the control condition.

Record anonymous participant ID, task, condition, order, completed (yes/no), time to resume in seconds, time spent re-reading above the saved position, repeated Submit/Pay actions, perceived effort (1–7), cue correct (yes/no/not applicable), and qualitative comments. Review with participants whether the next step was actually correct. Store no names or health details in the results.

Use `results.csv` for raw data. Report participant count and missing values; calculate completion percentage-point difference, paired median time difference, effort difference and cue accuracy denominator. With such a small sample, present exploratory results without broad or causal claims. Include any outcomes that worsen.

Current results: **0 participants; no measurements available.** Human recruitment, consent, interviews and observation cannot be substituted with automated browser tests.
